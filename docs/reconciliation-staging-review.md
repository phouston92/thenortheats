## Financial correctness

The launch policy stays £1 customer service fee and £1 partner fee on completed orders. Cash completion accrues £2 once. Card completion invoices only the partner fee; application-fee income/refunds are observed independently, never billed twice. Existing server price snapshots, completion/invoice/refund idempotency and cumulative penny/VAT reversal tests remain enabled.

Dashboard refunds use the same locked refund settlement and proportional immutable fee/tax credits. Pending/failure cannot credit money, duplicate/new-ID replays cannot multiply settlement, and a refund-before-capture defers until actual evidence arrives. Account/currency/amount mismatch cannot be silently assigned to another tenant.

Merchant balance transactions retain actual gross/fee/net and fee-detail evidence, not guessed processing rates. Payout pages link transactions once and track complete/net-match state; manual payouts remain unallocated. A dispute appends merchant exposure debit/reversal/correction, without inventing a second cash movement, platform invoice or tax liability. Active/lost chargebacks block new automatic refunds. Accounting/legal retention of platform fees on disputes and formal credit notes still require approved policy.

## Outstanding staging/production blockers

1. Private test credentials and real Standard Connect/onboarding/controller/fee evidence; actual card/3DS/failure/cancellation/refund/dispute/payout and webhook delivery tests. Live activation is still prohibited.
2. Unpatched mobile advisories, reviewed OS-image scans/digest provenance and independent application/security/financial review.
3. Hosted TLS/DNS/firewall/secret-manager validation; real email integrity/delivery; alert receivers/escalation ownership; off-host encrypted/PITR/Redis/key recovery and restore/failover drills with agreed RPO/RTO. Local validators are not those drills.
4. Full database RLS/composite coverage, granular partner roles, formal access reviews, idle timeout/retention/privacy controls and incident recovery procedures. The implemented fixed administrator scopes and tenant guards do not claim full multitenant DB isolation.
5. Actual dispute evidence/legal workflows, scheduled account-wide settlement import, larger-order pagination/manual payout matching and approved fee/tax/formal credit-note policies. Aged unbound ambiguous money operations remain intentionally blocked until positive evidence; no manual success switch exists.
6. A release and rollback review that preserves new administrator scope, keyring and financial reconciliation semantics. Older baseline images are not safe rollback targets for scoped/named-key data merely because SQL remains compatible.

Prepared for focused provider verification and a separately authorised staging review. **Not deployed, not live-payment ready, and not cleared for production traffic.**
