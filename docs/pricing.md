# Pricing and billing

No partner pricing is applied automatically. Policies are explicitly approved by an administrator, append-only, effective-dated, partner-specific and snapshotted on each order. The intended launch values are £1 customer service fee plus £1 completed-order partner fee. The seed creates pending partners only.

All monetary amounts are integer GBP pence. Rates are integer basis points. BigInt intermediate arithmetic rounds half away from zero for signed adjustments; cumulative proportional refund targets prevent repeated partial-refund penny drift. Commission is fixed fee plus rounded percentage of food subtotal, bounded by configured minimum/maximum. Percentage commission does not include delivery or the customer service fee in this baseline.

Cash completion accrues a partner fee and a cash customer-service-fee receivable, since the customer pays the takeaway directly. With launch values the platform ledger receives £2 before VAT. Card completion accrues only the partner fee: its customer service fee is already allocated as a Stripe application fee. Incomplete/rejected/cancelled orders accrue no completed-order fees. Completion and unique ledger references are transactional and retries cannot double bill.

Weekly periods are UTC Monday 00:00 to the following Monday, exclusive. Only closed periods generate invoices. Uninvoiced older entries carry forward, including fee credits, and a ledger row can be billed once. Invoice numbers use a database sequence; gaps after failed transactions are permitted. Existing draft invoices are reproducible and never acquire new lines on retry. Later adjustments appear on a future draft. Formal issuance, status management, credit-note documents and collection are pending. The admin generates drafts manually; a weekly scheduler is not implemented.

Tax rates and confirmation flags are explicit policy fields. The development zero-rate/unconfirmed fixture is not a UK VAT decision. The baseline snapshots configured line VAT from net amounts and cumulatively reverses the original tax on partial fee credits; whether advertised fees should be inclusive must be agreed and implemented before real billing. Drafts may contain unconfirmed tax; there is no issue/collect endpoint. Subscription values are stored for future design but nonzero subscriptions block checkout because subscription charging is not implemented. Promotions and loyalty are not active or implemented. There is no general global service-fee administration yet; partner policy approval controls service fee snapshots.

Partner-fee refunds are proportional to gross order refund amount, cumulatively rounded, only when the original fee was accrued. Cash service-fee repayment is not enabled. Financially consequential variations require explicit product/accountant approval; see open-decisions.md.

Stabilisation adds nullable immutable ledger `vat_amount` snapshots: new fee/credit entries specify the amount, while pre-existing NULL entries retain their original calculation. Invoicing preserves recorded tax rounding. Three partial £0.33/£0.34/£0.33 credits against a test £1 fee with 20p configured VAT reverse exactly 20p tax, rather than 21p. This illustrative test rate is not a launch VAT decision.
